DUNGEON SHOOTER / PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

Find your way. Fight your way.

A dungeon shooter. Defeat enemies, earn time, and descend.

Desktop keyboard + mouse · Click inside to play and enable audio.

Open game on its own ↗

01 / THE DESCENT

Choose your route.

  1. Reach the stairs before time runs out. Kills add time.
  2. Choose an upgrade after each floor.
  3. Every fifth floor is a boss fight with no timer.
  4. One hit restarts the floor. Your upgrades stay.
