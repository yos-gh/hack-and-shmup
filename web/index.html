HACK & SHMUP is a roguelite dungeon shooter. Descend floor by floor, break through enemy fire, and build your ship with upgrades along the way.

How to play

Goal

Reach the stairs before time runs out. Defeat enemies to earn more time, and choose upgrades as you descend.

Bosses

Face one of three bosses every five floors, with no time limit. Boss practice is available from the title screen.

Resume with a password

The pause screen shows a password for the current floor (click it to copy). Enter it with CONTINUE on the title screen to restart from the start of that floor.

Subweapons

  • Scatter: a close-range spread.
  • Shockwave: pushes enemies back and clears nearby bullets.
  • Lance: a piercing beam.
