DUNGEON SHOOTER / PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

Find your way. Fight your way.

Push deeper into an ever-changing dungeon.
Every kill buys time. Every floor offers a choice.

Desktop keyboard + mouse · Click inside to play and enable audio.

Open game on its own ↗

01 / THE DESCENT

Choose your route.

  1. Find the stairs before time runs out. Rush through, or lure enemies out and clear a path. Kills add time; clearing every room is optional.
  2. Grow stronger between floors. Choose an upgrade after each clear. Use your machine gun and three switchable subweapons together.
  3. Face a boss every fifth floor. Three distinct encounters, no timer. Defeat the boss to reveal the stairs.
  4. One hit. Another try. Retry the same floor immediately with your upgrades intact. Enemies and the timer reset.

Your deepest cleared floor lasts for this session. There is no permanent save.

03 / YOUR SECOND OPTION

Three ways to make space.

Scatter

A close-range spread. Get an angle and clear a cluster.

Shockwave

Push enemies back and erase nearby bullets. Use the opening to reposition.

Lance

A wide, piercing line. Cut through enemies lined up along your route.

Walls block attacks. Enter a room before firing into it, and flank shields to deal damage.

BOSS PRACTICE

Skip straight to the showdown.

Press B on the title screen. Choose a boss with ← / → and a floor with ↑ / ↓, then press Enter. Upgrades are supplied automatically. In practice, R retries and B returns to selection. Your session record is unaffected.

Having trouble playing?

This game is designed for a desktop keyboard and mouse; touch controls are not available. Click inside the game if keys do not respond. If audio is quiet, check the audio mode with M. You can also open the game directly. For local setup and Web build instructions, see the README.
